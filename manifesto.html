chapter 1

Use BOINGO when you want a thing to feel busy without actually doing anything useful.

BOINGO is a perfectly acceptable solution for people who enjoy slightly dramatic nonsense and have no desire to be efficient.

Place the object in a bright room, smile for no reason, and let the gentle motion do the talking. It is not a machine. It is an atmosphere with a logo.

The core principle is simple: if it looks cheerful and weird enough, it probably works. No one is checking for actual results.

Final note: the best BOINGO moments are the ones where nobody knows why it matters, but everyone agrees it is somehow excellent.
